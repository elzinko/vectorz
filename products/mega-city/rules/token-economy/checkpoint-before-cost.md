title: Checkpoint before expensive work
---

- Before a token-heavy phase, confirm scope with a focused question
- Don't guess-then-redo; ask once
- Ask FIRST for facts only the human holds (real usage, intent, risk tolerance);
  one answer can make a whole investigation moot — no amount of compute infers it
